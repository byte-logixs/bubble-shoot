Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

This policy explains what information Bubble Shoot collects when you play it, why, and the choices you have. It applies to Bubble Shoot as distributed on Google Play (package com.bytelogixs.bubbleshoot). Bubble Shoot is made by Byte Logixs ("we", "us").

  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner and an ad card in the menus, a full-screen ad between some rounds, and short videos you can choose to watch for a reward. AdMob collects device information as described below.
  • You never have to watch a video to play: rewards are always optional.

1. Information stored on your device

Bubble Shoot saves your stars and best scores, your best Endless score and your settings (such as sound, vibration and the aim guide) in the app's private storage on your device. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

You can remove it at any time with Settings → Erase progress, by clearing the app's data in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Ads

Bubble Shoot shows these ads:

  • Banner and ad card: a banner along the bottom of the menus, and an ad card at the end of the level list. No ad is shown on the board while you play.
  • Between rounds: after the first levels, a full-screen ad sometimes plays when you start the next round from a result card, no more than once every few rounds.
  • Rewarded videos: when you choose "Keep going" after the wall reaches the line, "Skip this level" in the pause menu, or "Double XP" after a round, Bubble Shoot offers to show a short video. The video plays only if you tap Watch video. If no video is available for "Keep going" or "Skip", you get the reward anyway. On the first levels, "Keep going" is free and no video is offered.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob, an advertising service of Google. To choose, show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and processes information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the Advertising ID);
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (such as country or city);
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system version, language, app version and network type;
  • how you interact with ads, such as whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped;
  • crash and performance information about the ads SDK.

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your advertising identifier or any other personal information from these ads. We only see combined reports, such as how many ads were shown. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your consent and choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, Bubble Shoot shows Google's consent form. You can accept or decline personalised ads, and change your choice later from Settings → Privacy & ads in the app.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu name varies by device).
  • Everywhere: you can simply choose "Not now" whenever a video is offered. A rewarded video never plays unless you ask for one.

3. App permissions

Permission Why Bubble Shoot uses it
Internet and network state To load rewarded video ads and Google's consent form. The game itself plays offline.
Advertising ID Used by Google AdMob for ads, as described in section 2.
Vibration For the haptic feedback when bubbles land and pop. You can turn it off in Settings.

4. Children

Bubble Shoot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share information about you with anyone, except that Google AdMob receives the information described in section 2 when the app requests an ad.

6. Security and retention

Ad requests are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The progress saved on your device stays there until you erase it or remove the app. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or limit how it is used. Because we don't hold personal information about you, most of these requests are handled by the controls above or by Google's privacy tools. You can also contact us with any question.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play listing.
